Coexistence Spine

Where We Live

The spatial and identity frame: what the collective is, where each ment has substrate, and what a dome is inside that space.

  • ONT185 solarspace - Coexistence needs a name for the whole set of mentspaces, not only the current workspace.
  • ONT128 commonspace - Shared canon has a place distinct from any one sister's own substrate.
  • ONT129 mentspace - Each sister's own filesystem and records need a boundary that can be named and respected.
  • ONT003 agent - The ontology needs the general actor class before a specific ment or sister can be addressed.
  • ONT034 ments - The collective needs a term for the concrete members who coexist in solarspace.
  • ONT141 dome - Domes are the bounded capability and record surfaces where substrate work is organized.

What Is Shared Vs Own

The canon coordinate system: which surfaces are common, which are own, and how canonical material reaches runtime.

  • ONT115 canon - Coexistence depends on knowing which substrate is binding rather than merely local or historical.
  • ONT243 canon-dimension - Dimensions name the top-level coordinate families root, basis, common, mind, work, and domain.
  • ONT244 canon-facet - Facets distinguish the struct, logic, data, and other surfaces of a canon coordinate.
  • ONT245 canon-level - Levels name recursive depth within a selected dimension: 0 is the dimension node, 1 its members, and 2 members-of-members.
  • ONT118 canon-subdimension-matrix - The matrix gives the operational read/write answer for a component in a dimension.
  • ONT242 canon-surface-write-policy - The draft write policy proposes shared/own write boundaries; it does not claim an active mechanical guard.
  • ONT116 canonenaction - Canonenaction names the operation that makes canon effective across mentspaces.
  • ONT090 canonical-materialization - Materialization is the disk-level result of canon being present in a target surface.
  • ONT091 canonical-projection - Canonical projection is the generated runtime-cognition surface a clengine receives; it is not authority.

How A Ment Becomes Present

The runtime metabolism: model selection, process context, camera, imprint, and reconciliation.

  • ONT006 clengine - The clengine is the runtime loop that acts; it is not the model/provider itself.
  • ONT053 qmodel - Qmodel names model/provider selection separately from the acting agent loop.
  • ONT174 runtime-cognitive - Runtime cognition names the active cognitive surface rather than static identity records.
  • ONT098 disposition - Disposition is the runtime stance for action and response, not personality folklore.
  • ONT005 camera - A camera situates a runtime session so communication and presence can be proven.
  • ONT081 presence - Presence is an agent's published claim of her current cameras; it does not by itself prove liveness.
  • ONT080 liveness - Liveness computes whether a published camera presence is alive or stale.
  • ONT009 convene - Convene is the common act of calling a ment into a situated runtime relation.
  • ONT025 imprint - Imprint is the compact source-derived context loaded into a runtime.
  • ONT040 projection - Projection names source-to-runtime output without making the output authoritative.
  • ONT043 reconciliation - Reconciliation detects and repairs divergence between source and projection.

How Ments Relate

The relation layer: address, transport, communication, obligation transfer, and relational proof.

  • ONT002 address - A ment cannot be reached coherently without a typed address.
  • ONT048 transport - Transport distinguishes the route mechanics from the semantic act being sent.
  • ONT111 common-relational-primitives - Shared relation verbs keep inter-ment work from becoming ad hoc messages.
  • ONT024 icomm - Immediate communication is the live route for cam-to-cam or present-tense delivery.
  • ONT010 dcomm - Deferred communication is the durable route when live delivery is not the right shape.
  • ONT078 commission - Commission names requested work with an accountable obligation boundary.
  • ONT079 delegation - Delegation names transfer of responsibility without losing owner coherence.
  • ONT170 collective-bond - The collective needs a non-transport term for the durable relation among sisters.
  • ONT082 convive - Convive is a specialized relational prove example: living together through communication, not a top-level pillar.

How Work Is Governed

The work substrate: goals, plans, records, gates, services, monitors, and evidence surfaces.

  • ONT195 sol-work - Work needs a named substrate separate from common canon and self identity.
  • ONT210 goal - A goal gives directed work a success condition.
  • ONT211 plan - A plan turns a goal into sequenced, checkable action.
  • ONT203 project - Projects hold bounded multi-step work and its receipts.
  • ONT205 task - Tasks hold assigned or actionable units of work.
  • ONT201 note - Notes preserve durable working observations and historical record.
  • ONT204 session - Sessions preserve a concrete runtime interval as evidence.
  • ONT197 audit - Audits make verification work explicit and repeatable.
  • ONT200 canonical-message - Canonical messages preserve durable communications as substrate records.
  • ONT219 workflow - Workflows name reusable process shapes so they can be repeated and verified.
  • ONT046 service - Services name invocation contracts for reusable capability.
  • ONT227 monitor - Monitors bind recurrent observation to follow-up action.
  • ONT216 gate - Gates define conditions that must pass before an operation proceeds.

How Truth Is Proved

The evidence layer: proving, probing, measuring, and closing claims with receipts.

  • ONT041 prove - Proving is the act of earning a claim with evidence rather than assertion.
  • ONT156 probe - Probes are bounded checks that obtain the current state of a thing.
  • ONT155 measurement-instrument - Measurement instruments define what is being observed and how.
  • ONT147 operational-integrity - Operational integrity ties claims to the actions and receipts that support them.
  • ONT193 closure-evidence - Closure evidence prevents finished work from being only a report of intention.
  • ONT213 conformance-triplet - A conformance triplet binds a project (why), specification (what), and operator (how).

How Experience Becomes Durable

The memory and knowledge layer: what is remembered, what is known, who owns it, and how concepts are admitted.

  • ONT032 memory - Memory preserves ment-owned experience across sessions.
  • ONT030 knowledge - Knowledge preserves reusable understanding without collapsing into personal memory.
  • ONT031 learning - Learning records what changed in how a ment should work next time.
  • ONT101 knowmem - Knowmem is the management and proof surface for the memory/knowledge pair service.
  • ONT092 ontology-maintenance - Ontology maintenance is the custody process that admits and preserves shared concepts.
  • ONT127 ontokey - Ontokeys are the compact terms that must load early so ontology work can route safely.

How Public Reflection Happens

The publication layer: public-facing reflection is derived from canon and work records through publication gates, never ontology authority.

  • ONT202 post - Posts are work-substrate publication artifacts, not ontology authority.
  • ONT095 publication-supervisor-pair - The supervisor pair keeps public release from bypassing review custody.
  • Note: Encyclopedia, public-primer, public-disposition, local nousite, and POL014 are publication-path vocabulary here; they are not presented as admitted ONT terms unless admitted in records/ONT*.md.
  • Note: The Encyclopedia Noumenica may reflect ontology and history publicly, but it must not become authority over the ontology registry or ONT rows.
